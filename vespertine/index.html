01 / The House

We only work with flowers that open at night.

Night flowers are not shy. They are loud. They have to be, because the moths that carry their pollen fly in the dark and cannot see a thing, so the scent has to do all the work.

Jasmine sambac opens at dusk and gives everything it has by midnight. We pick it then, in the dark, by hand, because by morning most of it is gone.
